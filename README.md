Comprehensive Sales & Operations Analysis
📌 Executive Summary
An end-to-end data analytics project evaluating regional sales drivers, profit margins, acquisition channels, and customer behavior across e-commerce datasets.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn): Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and data cleaning.

SQL (MySQL Workbench): Multi-table joins, data validation, and business logic queries.

Microsoft Power BI: Interactive dark-theme dashboard with custom KPIs, slicers, and structured layout.

Microsoft Excel & Power Query: Automated data transformation, VLOOKUP/merge replacements, and conditional columns.

📊 Key Insights & Observations
1. Marketing & Acquisition Channels
Organic Search is the Main Engine: Organic search drives the vast majority of total revenue (generating over $1.48M / 741 customers / 3,458 orders), far outperforming every other channel in sales volume[cite: 9].

Quality vs. Quantity: Secondary channels like paid search and referrals bring in higher-spending customers who buy larger or more expensive orders[cite: 9].

Affiliate Underperformance: Affiliate marketing generated only 93 customers and $122K in revenue[cite: 9].

2. Product Categories & Profitability
Furniture is the Profit King: Furniture brings in the highest actual dollar profit ($172,654.41) due to high price tags, despite lower total unit sales (1,045 units)[cite: 9].

Storage is the Hidden Goldmine: Storage boasts the highest profit margin percentage in the entire store at 55.79% (keeping over 55 cents of every dollar)[cite: 9].

Kitchen Moves Volume: Kitchen sells the highest number of units (4,006 units) and provides consistent customer traffic[cite: 9].

3. Store Operations & Revenue Leakage
Returns & Cancellations: Non-completed orders resulted in significant revenue loss ($221.7K returned + $143.6K canceled, totaling $365,346.16 in leakage across 751 orders)[cite: 9].

Free Shipping Impact: Free shipping drives massive order volume and basket size ($522.62 Average Order Value for free shipping compared to $81.85 for paid shipping)[cite: 9].

Discount Code Efficiency: Orders without discounts show a higher Average Order Value ($136) compared to discounted orders ($108), indicating that flat discounts currently reduce order value rather than encouraging larger basket sizes[cite: 9].

🚀 Strategic Recommendations
Protect & Shift Marketing Budget: Protect organic search traffic as the primary revenue driver, while shifting targeted acquisition budget toward paid search and referrals to capture high-spending customers[cite: 9].

Restructure Promotions: Replace flat discounts with minimum spend thresholds (e.g., "Spend $150 to get $20 off") to enforce larger basket sizes[cite: 9].

Optimize Operations & Packaging: Investigate shipping and packaging quality for bulky items (furniture) and fragile decor (mirrors and garlands) to reduce return frequencies and plug cash leaks[cite: 9].

Leverage Free Shipping Thresholds: Formalize a clear free-shipping threshold to incentivize customers to add higher-margin items to their carts[cite: 9].
